Privacy policy

Lecture Search for UCSD. Last updated November 7, 2026.

Lecture Search is a student project. It lets you search the lecture recordings of your own UCSD courses and ask questions about them. This page says what it reads, where that goes, and how to delete it.

What it reads

Only when you press Sync (or when you turn on daily sync for a course), and only from sites you are already signed in to:

  • From Canvas: the course code, name, term, instructors, sections and the names of course files.
  • From the course's Kaltura media gallery and from podcast.ucsd.edu: each lecture's title, date, length, link and captions.

It never reads or keeps your password, cookies or login sessions. It does not read your grades, messages, submissions or any site other than the four listed in its permissions.

Where it is stored

Everything it reads is stored in this browser only (Chrome's extension storage and IndexedDB on your computer). The search index is built on your computer by a small model that ships inside the extension. None of it is sent to us, and we run no server that stores your lectures.

What leaves your computer when you ask or study

To write an answer, a lesson, flashcards or test questions, the extension sends three things to our answer service:

  • your question (study tools send no question, only which of the three you asked for),
  • caption excerpts, each labeled with a lecture number and time. For a question: up to 20 short excerpts that matched it. For study tools: the captions of the one lecture you picked (up to 60 excerpts, which is all of a normal lecture),
  • a random ID made when the extension was installed, used only to limit how many questions can be asked per minute.

The answer service passes the question and excerpts to OpenRouter, which sends them to an AI model (currently OpenAI's GPT-4o mini) and returns the answer. The answer service does not store questions, excerpts or answers, and its logs hold only counts and status codes. OpenRouter and the model provider handle the request under their own privacy policies. No course names, file names, links or full transcripts are sent.

What we never do

  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data, or use it for anything except answering your question or making your study set.
  • We do not share one student's lectures with another student.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Open Lecture Search, pick a course and choose "Remove from this browser" to delete that course. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored.
