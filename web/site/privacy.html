Privacy

Last updated November 2026.

What we store

  • Your UCSD email address and name, from your Google sign-in.
  • The course material you sync with the extension: course details (code, name, term, instructors, sections), lecture titles, dates and captions, the list of course files, and the text of the slide files (PDF and PowerPoint) you sync. If you pick a course website, the text of its lecture pages.
  • Search data made from that material, so questions can find the right passages.
  • Your sign-in sessions, stored only as a one-way hash, and when you last signed in.

Each student's courses are stored separately. No other student can see yours.

What we don't store

  • Passwords. Google checks who you are; we never see your password.
  • Google access or refresh tokens, and anything else from your Google account.
  • Your Canvas cookies or Kaltura session keys. They stay in your browser.
  • Your questions and the answers. They are not saved or logged.

Where it is stored, and who else sees it

  • In a database on a server we run, which only this app can reach. Backups of it are kept for 7 days.
  • When you ask a question or use a study tool, the question and the passages found for it are sent to OpenRouter, which passes them to an AI model to write the answer. Scanned slide pages are sent the same way to be read.
  • Sign-in goes through Google.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and we use no ads or tracking.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account removes your email, name, sessions and every course, lecture, caption, slide and search entry stored for you, at once. Copies in backups are gone within 7 days. Signing out of the extension also deletes what it keeps in your browser.
