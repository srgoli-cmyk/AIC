Ask your lectures.

AI Lecture Search reads the captions and slides of your UCSD Canvas courses and answers your questions about them. Every answer links to the lecture and the minute it was said, or to the slide page.

Open the app

How it works

  • Sign in with your UCSD Google account. Only verified @ucsd.edu accounts can sign in.
  • Install the Chrome extension, open Canvas and click Sync. It copies the lecture captions and slide files of the courses you choose. You see only your own courses.
  • Ask a question, or turn a lecture into a lesson, flashcards or practice problems. Answers cite the lecture minute, so you can check them.

Your data

We store your UCSD email and name and the course material you sync, and nothing else. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. Read the privacy policy.

AI Lecture Search is a student project at UC San Diego. It is not run by UC San Diego.
